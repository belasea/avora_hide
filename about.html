    Our Story

    Born From a Love of Honest Materials

    AvoraHide began with a simple frustration: it was nearly impossible to find leather goods that were built to last without an inflated luxury markup. We set out to change that — sourcing full-grain and top-grain hides directly, and partnering with small workshops who still cut and stitch by hand.

    Today, every wallet, bag, belt, and jacket we sell carries that same founding idea: fewer, better things, made to be used — and to age beautifully while you use them.

    Our Craft

    Made with Passion. Built for Generations.

    Each hide is inspected by hand before it's cut, so every finished piece carries consistent grain and color. Edges are burnished rather than painted, stitching is doubled at stress points, and hardware is solid brass — details that rarely show up in a product photo, but always show up five years later.
